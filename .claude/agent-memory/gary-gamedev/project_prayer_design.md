Prayer's primary purpose is healing. The random cleanse/resistance pool is a bonus on top of that — not the main draw.

**Why:** Status effect management will come from other passives and specials the player acquires over time. Prayer is not meant to be the dedicated answer to status effects.

**How to apply:** Do not second-guess or redesign Prayer around its cleanse/resistance mechanic. Evaluate it as a healer first. Do not suggest making the status utility more prominent or reliable.
